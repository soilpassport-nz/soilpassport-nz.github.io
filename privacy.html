The short version

  • Soil Passport Ltd, a UK company, is responsible for your information.
  • We collect what we need to book and run soil tests, map and record your fields, give you soil and crop information, and run the farm programmes you join.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Your field and farm records are seen by you and by the people working with you on Soil Passport. Anything you post on the Ask board can be seen by everyone signed in.
  • Our servers and databases are in the European Union.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information. You can delete your account in the app under Menu → Profile → Delete account.
  • Questions? Email support@soilpassport.com.

01Who we are

Soil Passport is provided by Soil Passport Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17385128). Our registered office is Sustainable Workspaces, 5th Floor, County Hall, Belvedere Road, London SE1 7PB, United Kingdom.

In this policy, “Soil Passport”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Soil Passport Ltd. We are the controller of your personal information under UK data protection law, and the Data Fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for looking after it.

You can contact us about privacy at any time at support@soilpassport.com (please put “Privacy” in the subject line).

02What this policy covers

This policy applies when you:

  • use the Soil Passport app on Android or iOS, as a farmer, soil tester or field agent;
  • use the Soil Passport web console, for example as a member of a partner organisation;
  • visit soilpassport.com or contact us.

Some organisations use Soil Passport to run their own farm programmes, for example a farmer producer organisation, an NGO or a project developer. Where such an organisation uses your information for its own purposes, it is also responsible for that use and should give you its own privacy notice. Our Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies.

03Information we collect

Information you give us, or that is recorded with you

  • Account details: your name, mobile phone number, email address, preferred language and your role (for example farmer or soil tester). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Farmer profile: your village, block, district and state, and the organisation or programme you belong to. Some registration forms also ask for your gender and social category. These questions are optional. Where we keep the answers, we use them only in totals, to report how inclusive a programme is.
  • Identity checks: some programmes need to confirm who you are, for example before paying you. In India we may do this with your Aadhaar number, through a licensed verification provider. The provider receives your Aadhaar number and the one-time password (OTP) you enter. We keep only the last four digits and whether the details matched. We never store your full Aadhaar number. We may also ask for a UPI ID so that we can pay you.
  • Fields and farm records: field names, field boundaries you walk or draw on the map, GPS location and accuracy, area, crops, irrigation and sowing dates. Your farm history can include seasons, yields, selling prices, input costs, fertiliser, spray and organic inputs, crop losses, irrigation and groundwater levels, and any photos you add. For carbon and biochar programmes, it also includes biochar applied and estimated carbon results.
  • Soil information: soil test results (such as pH, nutrients and organic carbon), and where and when the sample was taken. It also includes soil photos, with the phone model and operating system version they were taken on, and the serial number of a soil probe if you connect one.
  • Bookings, payments and payouts: soil test bookings (date, place, tester, price, payment method and status) and your carbon token and programme payment records. Card and online payments are handled by our payment providers, and we never see your full card details. If we pay you as a soil tester, we also keep your payout details (for example the email address of your PayPal account) and your payout history.
  • Ask board: questions, answers and photos you post, and any posts you report. Before storing a photo, we remove hidden information such as the location it was taken.
  • Messages: anything you send us, for example an email to support.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical logs: when the app, console or website connects to our servers, our hosting providers log your IP address, the time and the feature or page requested. We use these logs to keep the service secure and to fix problems.
  • Location: while you use features that need it, such as walking a field boundary or tagging a field or soil photo. We do not track your location in the background.
  • On your phone: the app saves your sign-in, language and unsent drafts in encrypted storage on the phone, so it keeps working without signal. See our Cookie Policy.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does not use your phone’s advertising ID.

Information from other people

  • Field agents, soil testers and partner organisations may register you and record your fields, soil samples or farm history for you. They must tell you what they are recording and get your agreement first.
  • Google tells us your name and email address when you sign in with Google.
  • Identity verification and payment providers tell us the result of a check or payment.

If you only visit our website

You do not need to give us any personal information to browse soilpassport.com. Our web host keeps standard server logs. If you email us or fill in a form, we keep what you send so that we can reply.

05Who can see your information

  • You. You can see your profile, fields, soil reports, bookings and token balance in the app.
  • People working with you on Soil Passport: the partner organisation running your programme and its field agents, and the soil tester you book. A tester sees your name, phone number, village and field location so that they can reach you.
  • Our staff and contractors, when they need to, to run and support the service.
  • Other Soil Passport users see only what you choose to make public. Questions, answers and photos on the Ask board show your name and can be seen by everyone signed in to Soil Passport. If you are a soil tester, farmers looking for a tester see your name, rating and service area.
  • Programme partners: if you join a carbon or biochar programme, we may share the records it needs (field boundaries, practices, biochar applied, soil results) with independent verifiers and carbon registries, so that results can be checked and certified. Your programme agreement explains what is shared. We do not give your phone number to credit buyers.
  • Service providers who help us run Soil Passport (see section 6). They may use your information only on our instructions.
  • When the law requires it, for example to answer a valid request from a court, regulator or the police, or to protect someone’s safety or our legal rights.
  • If our business changes hands, for example in a merger or sale. Whoever takes over must protect your information in line with this policy.
  • When you choose to share, for example when you send your soil report from your phone.

07Where your information is stored

Our database, servers and file storage are in data centres in the European Union. The UK recognises the EU as giving adequate protection to personal information. Information about farmers in India is also stored there, which Indian law permits.

Some providers listed above, such as Google, Stripe, PayPal and Namecheap, may process information in other countries, including the United States. When personal information protected by UK law leaves the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations, including the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Otherwise we use the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Contact us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

08How long we keep it

  • Your account: for as long as it is open.
  • When you delete your account: if there is nothing else on file, we delete your account straight away. If you have fields, soil tests, payments, carbon tokens or Ask board posts, we switch your account off and keep those records. Programme partners, payment and tax rules and carbon registries need them to stay complete and accurate. You can ask us to delete or anonymise them where the law allows.
  • Payment and accounting records: six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK tax law requires.
  • Carbon and biochar programme records: for as long as the programme and the registry require, which can be many years after credits are issued.
  • Soil reports: at least one year. Stored reports are locked for a year so that they cannot be altered or lost.
  • Sign-in sessions: they end 30 days after you last use the app.
  • Technical logs: usually about 30 days, unless we need them longer to investigate a security problem.
  • Support messages: up to two years after the conversation ends.

After these periods we delete information, or anonymise it so that it no longer identifies you.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it (see section 8);
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including use based on legitimate interests;
  • take information you gave us to another service, in a common machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email support@soilpassport.com, or ask your field agent to contact us for you. You can delete your account yourself in the app under Menu → Profile → Delete account. Using your rights is free. We may need to confirm who you are first, and we will reply within one month. If a request is complex, we may extend this by up to two more months, and we will tell you if we do.

10If you are in India

This section applies to you if you use Soil Passport in India. It explains your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

  • Consent. We use your personal data with the consent you give when you register, or for other uses the Act allows. Field agents should explain this notice to you before you register.
  • Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account or contacting us, as easily as you gave it. We will then stop using your data unless the law requires us to keep it. Some services may stop working.
  • Your rights. You can ask for a summary of the personal data we hold, how we use it, and who we have shared it with. You can also ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it. You can nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or are unable to act.
  • Aadhaar. We use Aadhaar only to verify your identity, with your consent, through a licensed provider. We never store or display your full Aadhaar number.
  • Grievances. To raise a concern, email support@soilpassport.com with “Grievance” in the subject line. We will respond as quickly as we can, and within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Your language. You can ask for this notice to be explained to you in Hindi or Odia.

12Keeping your information safe

We protect your information with technical and organisational measures that fit the risk. These include:

  • encryption of all information between the app and our servers;
  • encrypted storage of your sign-in and drafts on your phone;
  • sign-in tokens stored on our servers only in scrambled (hashed) form;
  • access controls that limit each person to what their role needs;
  • removing hidden location data from photos before they are stored.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell the relevant regulator and, where required, tell you, as the law requires.

13Children

Soil Passport is for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect information about children. If you believe a child’s information has been given to us, contact us and we will delete it.

14Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
