The short version

  • We use only what is needed to make our website, console and app work, and to remember your choices.
  • We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.
  • Anything that is not essential stays off until you agree to it, and you can change your mind at any time.

01What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves in your browser. Websites can also save information in your browser’s local storage, and apps can save it on your phone. In this policy we call all of these “cookies”, because the same rules apply to them.

Some cookies are strictly necessary: without them a service cannot work, for example to keep you signed in. The law allows these without your consent. All other cookies, such as those that remember preferences or measure visits, need your consent first.

02Our approach

  • We use strictly necessary cookies to run our services and to remember your cookie choices.
  • We use optional cookies only if you allow them, and we keep them to a minimum.
  • We do not use advertising, retargeting or social media tracking cookies, and we do not sell information collected through cookies.

03On soilpassport.com

When you first visit, a banner asks which optional cookies you allow. Until you choose, only strictly necessary storage is used.

05In the app

The Soil Passport app does not use browser cookies. It saves a small amount of information in encrypted storage on your phone so that it works properly, including without signal:

  • your sign-in, so that you don’t have to sign in every time;
  • your role, language and a short summary of your profile;
  • drafts and field locations that have not yet been sent, so that nothing is lost while you are offline.

The app contains no advertising or analytics tools, and does not read your phone’s advertising ID. Deleting the app removes this information from your phone.

06Third-party services

Some features rely on other companies. They may set their own cookies or receive your IP address, under their own policies:

  • Sign in with Google (app and console). Google may use cookies on its own sign-in pages. See Google’s cookie policy.
  • Google Fonts (console). Your browser downloads fonts from Google’s servers, which see your IP address. No cookies are set.
  • OpenStreetMap (app). Map images come from OpenStreetMap’s servers, which see your IP address and the area you are viewing. No cookies are set.
  • Payment providers (Stripe and PayPal), when you pay online. They may use cookies on their payment pages to process the payment and prevent fraud.

07Your choices

To change your choices on soilpassport.com, use the cookie settings link in the website footer, or clear this site’s data in your browser and we will ask again.

You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge. If you block strictly necessary storage, parts of our services, such as signing in to the console, will not work.

08Changes and contact

We will update this policy whenever we change the cookies we use. The date at the top shows when it last changed. For questions, email support@soilpassport.com or write to Soil Passport Ltd, Sustainable Workspaces, 5th Floor, County Hall, Belvedere Road, London SE1 7PB, United Kingdom.
