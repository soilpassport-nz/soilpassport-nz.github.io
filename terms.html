04What we provide

Depending on your role and where you are, Soil Passport lets you:

  • book soil tests and receive soil reports;
  • map your fields and keep a history of crops, inputs, yields and costs;
  • see soil, crop and weather information for your fields;
  • ask and answer questions on the Ask board;
  • take part in farm programmes, such as carbon and biochar projects, run by us or by partner organisations;
  • for soil testers, field agents and organisations, manage bookings, farmers and projects.

Using the app is free unless a price is shown before you buy something. Some features are new or in pilot and are marked as such, or as “coming soon”. They may change as we improve them.

05Soil test bookings and payment

  • Price. The app shows the price of a soil test, including any taxes, before you book. That is the price you pay.
  • Paying. You can pay in the ways the app offers when you book. These may include paying the soil tester in cash on the day, or paying online through a payment provider such as Stripe or PayPal. If you pay cash, the tester records it in the app.
  • The test. A soil tester will contact you to arrange a visit, collect a sample from the field you chose, and record it. Your report appears in the app when the results are ready.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel a booking free of charge at any time before the sample is taken. If you have already paid, we will give you a full refund.
  • If something goes wrong. If we or the tester cannot carry out the test, or the report is faulty, we will repeat the test at no cost or give you a full refund. You choose which.
  • Refunds go back to the way you paid, within 14 days of our agreeing to them. Cash payments are refunded to you directly or by UPI.
  • UK consumers normally have 14 days to cancel a service. When you book, you ask us to carry out the test within that period. If you cancel after the sample has been taken, we may charge for the work already done. Once the report has been delivered, the right to cancel ends.

06Soil results, advice and weather

Soil reports, crop advice, weather forecasts and other information in Soil Passport help you make your own decisions. Soil results come from a sample of your field and from testing methods with natural limits. Weather forecasts come from a third-party service and can be wrong.

We take reasonable care to make this information accurate, but we cannot promise any particular harvest, yield, income or outcome. Before acting, use your own judgement and, where it matters, local expert advice. Always follow the label and local rules when using fertilisers, pesticides or other inputs.

07Farm programmes, carbon and biochar

  • Separate agreements. Carbon, biochar and other farm programmes have their own agreement with you, for example a memorandum of understanding (MOU). That agreement sets out who can take part, which practices you follow, how results are checked, who owns any carbon credits, and how, when and how much you may be paid. If it differs from these terms, the programme agreement applies.
  • Carbon tokens are records, not money. Tokens and balances in the app record estimated carbon results and programme rewards. They are not money, not carbon credits, and not an investment or security. They cannot be sold, transferred or exchanged for cash, except where your programme agreement expressly says so.
  • Estimates can change. Carbon figures shown before measurement and independent verification are estimates. They may go up or down once results are checked. We will correct any mistakes we find.
  • No guarantees. We cannot promise that a programme will produce carbon credits, that credits will be issued by a registry, or that they will sell at any price.
  • Accurate records. Programme results depend on honest, accurate records. If records are false or misleading, we or the programme partner may remove them, reverse any tokens or rewards based on them, and end your participation.
